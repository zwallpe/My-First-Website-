What is Fantasy Football?

Fantasy Football is a game where you act as the general manager of a virtual team made up of real NFL players. Each week, your players earn points based on their actual performance in real-life NFL games.

How You Win Points

  • Passing Yards: 1 point per 25 yards
  • Passing Touchdown: 4 points
  • Rushing / Receiving Yards: 1
