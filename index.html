Welcome to Fantasy Football

Learn the basics, draft your dream team, and manage your roster like an NFL General Manager.

1. Draft Your Team

Every fall, fans join leagues and hold a "draft" to pick real NFL players (QBs, RBs, WRs, TEs, Kickers, and Defenses) to build their custom roster.

2. Matchup Each Week

Your team competes 1-on-1 against another manager in your league. When your real-life players score yards and touchdowns in actual NFL games, your team earns fantasy points.

3. Win Your League

Set your starting lineup each week, make strategic trades, and pickup free agents. The managers with the best records make the playoffs to fight for the Championship!
