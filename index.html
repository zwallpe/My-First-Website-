Welcome to Fantasy Football! 🏈

New to the game? Don't worry! Fantasy football transforms real-life NFL statistics into an exciting competitive game between you and your friends. Here is everything you need to know to play like a pro.

✓ Quick 3-Min Read ✓ Standard & PPR Scoring Rules ✓ Interactive Point Simulator
1 What is Fantasy Football?

You act as a team's General Manager (GM) and Head Coach. You draft real NFL players (like Patrick Mahomes or CeeDee Lamb) to build your roster. Each week during the NFL season, you set your lineup and compete head-to-head against another league member.

2 How Do You Win?

When your players stats occur in real Sunday games, they earn points for your fantasy team! For example: 10 rushing yards = 1 point, 1 touchdown = 6 points. Whichever fantasy owner scores more total points at the end of Monday night wins that week's matchup!

3 Essential Roster Spots

Standard starting lineups usually consist of:

  • 1 QB: Quarterback (Passer)
  • 2 RB: Running Backs (Runners)
  • 2 WR: Wide Receivers (Catchers)
  • 1 TE: Tight End (Hybrid)
  • 1 FLEX: Extra RB, WR, or TE
  • 1 Defense/Kicker: Team D/ST & K

💡 Beginner Draft Cheat Code
1. RB & WR Value in Early Rounds: Top running backs and wide receivers score the most consistent non-QB points. Secure 3 to 4 strong RBs and WRs in your first 4 draft rounds.
2. Don't Draft a Kicker Early: Wait until the final round for Kickers and Defenses. Their year-to-year performance is unpredictable and easy to swap during the season.
3. Know "Bye Weeks": Every NFL team takes 1 week off during the season. Avoid drafting too many key players who share the exact same Bye Week!
4. "Handcuffing": Drafting the backup running back to your star starter protects your team if your top player suffers an injury.
